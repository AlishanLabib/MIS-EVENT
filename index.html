Guest Speaker Session

Digital Transformation in Healthcare in Bangladesh

Date: Tuesday, May 6, 2025
Time: 1:30 PM - 3:00 PM
Venue: S. M. NOUSHER ALI (Lecture Gallery - 126)

Resource Person:

Mr. Parvez Ahmad

Chief Operating Officer, CliniCall Limited, Dhaka.

Guest Lecture Objectives:

  • Understand the Concept of Digital Transformation: Introduce what digital transformation means in the context of healthcare and how it differs from simple digitization.
  • Explore Key Technologies: Provide an overview of transformative technologies such as Electronic Health Records (EHRs), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Telemedicine, Internet of Medical Things (IoMT), Blockchain, and Big Data Analytics.
  • Share Real-World Applications: Share case studies or examples of how digital transformation is being successfully implemented in hospitals, clinics, and public health systems.
  • Identify Benefits and Challenges: Discuss the potential improvements in patient care, operational efficiency, and decision-making, as well as challenges like data privacy, security, and digital literacy.
  • Highlight Trends and Future Directions: Explore emerging trends such as personalized medicine, wearable health tech, virtual care models, and the role of AI in diagnostics and treatment planning.
  • Foster Interdisciplinary Collaboration: Emphasize the need for collaboration between healthcare professionals, IT specialists, data scientists, and policymakers for successful digital integration.
